BUFFALO SOLDIERS · ISSUE #1BY MYIA

The War of the Unheard Voice

Inside every person is an army. Mine has eight soldiers. This is how they fight for my voice.

CHAPTER 1

Meet the Army

Four soldiers guard my heart. Four show up with me at work. Each one has a power, and each one has a crack in the armor.

THE PERSONAL SQUAD

Steven

COMPASSION

Steven reads the room and feels what others feel. He defends the people who get overlooked and always looks for the peaceful way through.

  • Empathetic
  • Protective
  • Peacemaker
  • Loyal
STRENGTH
Sees every side and calms conflict before it grows.
WEAKNESS
Puts everyone else first and hides his own needs.

Amethyst

AUTHENTICITY

Amethyst refuses to pretend. She brings the jokes when things get heavy and reminds me I don't have to be perfect to be worthy.

  • Genuine
  • Funny
  • Resilient
  • Bold
STRENGTH
Keeps it real and lightens the room.
WEAKNESS
When she feels judged, insecurity makes her act out or shrink.

Pearl

PREPARATION

Pearl plans, organizes, and remembers every detail. With her around, I'm never caught off guard.

  • Organized
  • Detail-oriented
  • Disciplined
  • Graceful
STRENGTH
Walks in with every point ready.
WEAKNESS
Perfectionism. She waits for the perfect moment, and it never comes.

Connie

DETERMINATION

Connie studies, trains, and shows up ready. When it matters, she has the courage to step in.

  • Studious
  • Courageous
  • Driven
  • Reliable
STRENGTH
Rehearses until she's confident, then follows through.
WEAKNESS
Carries a lot of pressure to always be the best.
THE PROFESSIONAL SQUAD

Garnet

COMPOSURE

Garnet stays steady when everything around her gets loud. She doesn't panic, and her calm makes people trust her.

  • Steady
  • Confident
  • Unshakable
  • Trusted
STRENGTH
Calm confidence under pressure.
WEAKNESS
Guards her feelings so closely that people find her hard to read.

Bismuth

ADVOCACY

Bismuth speaks up when something isn't fair, for me and for others. She builds things that last and won't let anyone get pushed aside.

  • Outspoken
  • Fair
  • Builder
  • Brave
STRENGTH
Names the problem out loud.
WEAKNESS
Her intensity can come on too strong and turn all-or-nothing.

Peridot

PROBLEM-SOLVING

Peridot breaks a challenge into pieces and figures out how to fix it. She's honest, curious, and always learning.

  • Analytical
  • Curious
  • Honest
  • Growing
STRENGTH
Spots patterns and builds real fixes.
WEAKNESS
Blunt, and her pride makes it hard to admit when she's wrong.

Sapphire

STRATEGY

Sapphire thinks several moves ahead and weighs every outcome before acting. She keeps my decisions grounded.

  • Foresighted
  • Level-headed
  • Patient
  • Logical
STRENGTH
Sees the interruption coming before it happens.
WEAKNESS
So focused on logic she can seem detached from feelings.
CHAPTER 2

The War Is Declared

It happens in every full staff meeting.

"So I think we could try a new approach to the onboarding—"
"ACTUALLY, WHAT IF WE—"
My voice gets lost. My ideas get talked over, or picked up later by someone else. I leave the room feeling unseen.
THE WARI'm constantly interrupted when I try to share ideas in full staff meetings. THE GOALGet my ideas fully heard, while keeping my professionalism and my relationships intact.
CHAPTER 3

The Battle Plan

The army moves in three phases: before the meeting, during the interruption, and after.

1

Before the Meeting

Preparation wins half the battle.

CONNIE

"Say it out loud three times. By the meeting, it'll feel like mine."

SAPPHIRE

"Same agenda item, same person, same moment. I know when the interruption is coming. Speak first on that item."

2

The Interruption

The battlefield. Calm, clear, and kind.

BISMUTH

"I'd like to finish my thought, and then I'd love to hear yours."

3

After the Meeting

Follow-through turns one win into a pattern.

STEVEN

One-on-one, assuming good intent: "I know you get excited about ideas. I do too. I'd love to finish my points next time."

CHAPTER 4

Battle Formations

When two soldiers fuse, they become something stronger than either one alone.

STEVONNIE = Steven + Connie. Compassion plus courage: I speak up without making anyone feel attacked.
VS
GARNET = Ruby + Sapphire. Passion plus strategy. My strategist is literally part of my composure.

GARNET + BISMUTH

Calm and assertive. Bismuth brings the words, Garnet keeps them steady. I take back the floor without raising my voice.

PEARL + SAPPHIRE

The War Room. Prep and strategy, done before I ever walk in.

Support each otherPull in different directions
CHAPTER 5

Support and Clash

An army doesn't always agree. First, how my soldiers hold each other up. Then, the moments they work against each other.

WHEN THEY SUPPORT EACH OTHER

  • Pearl trains Connie, so courage comes with a plan.
  • Garnet lends Bismuth her calm, so the fire lands well.
  • Sapphire feeds Pearl the strategy, so the prep is aimed at the right moment.
  • Amethyst reminds Pearl she doesn't have to be perfect to speak.
  • Steven keeps Bismuth's words kind, so the relationship survives the battle.
WHEN THEY DON'T: Under pressure, my soldiers don't always agree. Some want to keep the peace, some want to fight back, and some freeze. Here's what happens when they pull against each other.

STEVEN

"Just let it go. It's not worth the tension."

BISMUTH

"If we let it go, it happens again next week."

AMETHYST

"Maybe my idea wasn't that good anyway."

SAPPHIRE

Overthinks every possible response, and the moment passes.

CHAPTER 6

Armor Up

Some soldiers need more protection than others. Here's who needs armor and where they get it.

Steven

Needs armor of self-worth, so his own needs count too.

From: my mentors and my i.c.stars cohort.

Amethyst

Needs confidence when insecurity creeps in.

From: teammates who affirm my contributions.

Pearl

Needs permission to be "good enough."

From: practicing speaking before I feel 100% ready.

Bismuth

Needs patience, so her fire lands well.

From: Garnet, who lends her calm.

Connie, Garnet, Peridot, and Sapphire are already well armored for this war. Connie's armor is Pearl's training. Garnet's is the promise between Ruby and Sapphire. Peridot's is the team that taught her to grow. Sapphire's is Ruby, who reminds her to feel.
CHAPTER 7

Maps for War and Peace

Where every soldier stands when the meeting turns into a battle, and where they rest when it doesn't.

WAR MAP. Garnet takes command. Bismuth and Connie hold the front line. Pearl and Sapphire run strategy from the War Room. Peridot moves in after the battle.
PEACE MAP. My ideas are heard, so the army stands down. Steven and Amethyst lead with connection and humor, and the rest recharge for the next meeting.
EPILOGUE

Victory

I finished my thought. My idea was heard. And I didn't lose myself, or my relationships, to get there. Every soldier had a part: Pearl prepared me, Sapphire saw it coming, Garnet kept me calm, Bismuth and Connie held the line, Amethyst kept it real, Steven protected the bond, and Peridot made sure it doesn't happen again.

THE END
